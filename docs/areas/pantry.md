# Pantry (home plot, back-middle area)

Stores the food the pond, the garden and the kitchen make; no workers. Its level is the food cap.
Built directly in Studio in the classic stud look.

## Level 1 (built: `tools/map/build_pantry_l1.luau`, picture `pantry-l1.jpg`)

| Piece | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Yard | a 24 x 20 grey stone stud yard with a darker border | the pantry's island |
| Shed | 11 x 8 honey-plank walls, chunky dark corner posts, a terracotta pitched roof with a ridge, plank gables | the hero piece |
| Door | dark double door with a gold ring, a stone step, a lantern beside it | where food goes in (later: slimes carry food to it) |
| Windows | a warm round window on each side | |
| Stock | three crates (carrots, fish, cabbages), two barrels with iron hoops, two grain sacks | "a full pantry looks full": later the stock shown grows with the food stored |
| Signpost | box icon, at the front | tap: level, food stored / cap, upgrade |
| Dressing | Nature stylized Pack: bushes, fern, tufts, flowers | |

## Later levels (one change each level, remodel at 4 / 7 / 10)

- Level 2-3: more crates and barrels, a shelf rack against the wall.
- Level 4: a sturdier storehouse with a stone cellar door at its side.
- Level 7: a two-storey storehouse with a hayloft door and a hanging pulley.
- Level 10: a big round granary with a cone roof and a weather vane.

Idea for gameplay: the stock props in the yard switch on in steps with how full the pantry is
(empty, a quarter, half, full), so the player reads their food at a glance without any UI.
