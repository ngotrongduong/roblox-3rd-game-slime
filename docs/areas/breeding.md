# Breeding pen (home plot, middle-right area)

The owner's design (2026-10-27): a simple rectangular pen with a fence frame; inside, a few nests
like hen's nests; beside it, a few egg incubators. The round pink "cupcake" cottage of the first try
stays as the pen's house; only the surroundings changed and a fence now closes the area.

## How it plays

1. The player moves slimes into the pen (through the gate).
2. Inside, they pair up by themselves; after a while a pair lays an egg in a free nest.
3. The egg is moved to a free incubator.
4. After a set time the egg hatches into a new slime (it then grows up, as in Phase 1).

Nests and incubators are the pen's capacity: no free nest, no new egg; no free incubator, the egg
waits in its nest. Slimes are not part of the build (the owner: slimes come later; no stand-ins).

- Level 4: a bigger pen and a covered nesting box row.
