# Breeding house (home plot, middle-right area)

Where the two chosen slimes go to make an egg. Level 1 holds one pair. Built directly in Studio in
the classic stud look.

## Level 1 (built: `tools/map/build_breeding_l1.luau`, picture `breeding-l1.jpg`)

| Piece | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Yard | a 24 x 22 soft pink stud yard with a cream border | |
| Cottage | a round cream cottage on a stud base, a pink band under the eaves, a stepped roof of five pink discs (the stud way of a cone), a red heart on top | the hero piece; reads like a cupcake |
| Door | arched dark-wood door with a gold knob, a stud step, a red heart above it | |
| Windows | two round warm windows on the curve of the wall | |
| Pair spots | two straw mats in front of the door, turned a little toward each other, Attachments `BreedSpot1` / `BreedSpot2` | where the pair stands while breeding |
| Love sparkles | a slow emitter of pink sparkles rising between the two spots | on while a pair breeds (gameplay switches it) |
| Flower pots | two terracotta pots of pink and white flowers by the door | |
| Signpost | heart icon | tap: pick the pair, see the egg's chances, upgrade |
| Dressing | four flowering bushes round the cottage, flowers, a tuft, a fern | |

## Later levels (pairs at once: 1, 1, 2, 3 at levels 1, 4, 7, 10)

- Level 2-3: a bench for waiting slimes, a bell by the door.
- Level 4: a bigger cottage with a little porch.
- Level 7: a two-room house with a small flower garden; a second pair of spots.
- Level 10: an elegant small manor with a rounded pink roof and a gentle fountain; a third pair.
